Carmen Andrade, a conjoined twin, quietly married her boyfriend of four years, Daniel McCormack, in October 2024. The couple got married on Lover’s Leap Bridge in New Milford, Connecticut, with only close family members around them.

Carmen and Daniel first met on the dating app Hinge in 2020. Carmen said she received many messages from men who were interested in her because of her condition.

"I got a lot of messages from guys with fetishes," she said. "I knew right off the bat that Daniel was different... he didn't lead with a question about my condition."

Carmen and her twin sister, Lupita, are joined at the torso. They share a pelvis, circulatory system, digestive system, and reproductive system, while each has her own heart and lungs.

Lupita has said she does not want to get married herself. She sees Daniel as someone she loves "as a brother."

Carmen and Daniel have explained that they handle their relationship and private life through constant communication and by respecting Lupita’s boundaries.

"If her sister isn't comfortable with something," Carmen said, "we just respect that."

Their relationship has required understanding, communication, and respect for everyone involved.
